## Message map
- Before: The demo spec says the RFI showed several platforms could support the modernization. The demonstration now has to prove practical fit on seven scenarios, in one order, on a global professional-services site.
- Required capabilities: Author and publish across sites, languages and brands with governance; Manage and reuse media, and say what is a separate DAM; Give authors AI help with human review; Help visitors find lawyers, expertise, locations and content; Keep content discoverable for SEO and AEO; Show performance and search insight to authors and marketers; Let a business user govern personalization and its fallback
- After: A scored walkthrough in the spec's order. The public site carries the visitor half. Authoring, DAM, AI operations, SEO controls, analytics and personalization rules are named where they are not pages on dentons.com.
- Metrics: "Spec weights: authoring 25%; AI operations 10%; search 10%; personalization 10%; analytics 8%; DAM 7%; SEO/AEO 5%"
- Differentiators: "The spec requires each scenario to say core licence; add-on; third party; custom or roadmap"
- Proof points: none in the demo spec
- Why now: The October/November 2026 spec is the evaluation script for the product demonstration.
